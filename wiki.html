What Are Complex Numbers?

Introduction

A complex number is a number that can be expressed in the form \(a + bi\), where \(a\) and \(b\) are real numbers, and \(i\) is the imaginary unit. \(a\) is called the real part, and \(b\) is called the imaginary part.

Imaginary Unit

The imaginary unit \(i\) represents \(\sqrt{-1}\), so \(i^2 = -1\).

Complex Plane

A complex number \(z = a + bi\) can be plotted on the complex plane, where the horizontal \(x\)-axis is the real axis and the vertical \(y\)-axis is the imaginary axis. Its modulus and argument are given by:

\[ r = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}, \qquad \tan\theta = \frac{b}{a} \]
